SECTION 07 9200 - JOINT SEALANTS PART 1 - GENERAL 1.1 SUMMARY A. Section includes sealants for the following joints: 1. Exterior joints in vertical surfaces and horizontal non-traffic surfaces. 2. Exterior joints in horizontal traffic surfaces. 3. Interior joints in vertical surfaces and horizontal non-traffic surfaces. 4. Interior joints in wet areas. 1.2 ACTION SUBMITTALS A. Product data: for each joint sealant product. B. Samples for colour selection: cured sample strips showing the full range of colours. C. Joint sealant schedule: include the following for each joint type: 1. Joint sealant application, joint location and designation. 2. Joint sealant manufacturer and product name. 3. Joint sealant formulation. 4. Joint sealant colour. 1.3 INFORMATIONAL SUBMITTALS A. Qualification data: for the installer. B. Field adhesion test reports: for each sealant and substrate combination tested. 1.4 QUALITY ASSURANCE A. Installer: a firm employing applicators trained by the sealant manufacturer. B. Mockups: install sealant joints in mockups of assemblies specified in other Sections. 1.5 FIELD CONDITIONS A. Do not proceed with installation under the following conditions: 1. When ambient and substrate temperatures are outside the limits permitted by the manufacturer, or below ___ deg F. 2. When joint substrates are wet. 3. Where joint widths are less than those allowed by the manufacturer. 1.6 WARRANTY A. Installer's warranty: replace sealants that fail to seal within ___ years from Substantial Completion. B. Cleaners for nonporous surfaces: chemical cleaners that do not stain or damage the surface. PART 3 - EXECUTION 3.1 PREPARATION A. Clean joints immediately before installing sealants. 1. Remove laitance and form-release agents from concrete. 2. Remove loose particles and dust by brushing or blowing with oil-free air. B. Prime joint substrates where recommended by the manufacturer. 3.2 INSTALLATION A. Install sealant backings of the type indicated to support sealants during tooling. B. Install sealants to fill joints completely, wetting the joint surfaces. C. Tool sealants immediately after application to form a smooth, concave joint. 3.3 FIELD QUALITY CONTROL A.
