SECTION 08 7100 - DOOR HARDWARE PART 1 - GENERAL 1.1 SUMMARY A. Section includes: 1. Hinges and continuous hinges. 2. Mechanical locks and latches. 3. Exit devices. 4. Door closers. 5. Stops, holders and protective plates. 6. Weatherstripping and thresholds. B. Related requirements: 1. Section 08 1416 "Flush Wood Doors" for factory-prepared wood doors. 2. Section 28 1300 "Access Control" for card readers and their wiring. 1.2 SUBMITTALS A. Product data: for each type of hardware item, showing finish and options selected. B. Hardware schedule: organized by hardware set, listing each opening served. 1. Show the location of each item on each door. 2. Coordinate the schedule with door and frame shop drawings. a. Include keying information on a separate sheet. C. Keying schedule: prepared after a meeting with the Owner, showing each key group. D. Samples: for each exposed finish, 4 inches square. 1.3 QUALITY ASSURANCE A. Installer qualifications: a firm with at least five years of experience installing similar hardware. B. Hardware consultant: a person certified as an architectural hardware consultant, available during the Work. C. Fire-rated openings: hardware listed for use on rated doors of the rating shown. 1.4 DELIVERY, STORAGE AND HANDLING A. Deliver each item packed and labeled with its hardware set number. B. Store hardware in a locked, dry room on site. PART 2 - PRODUCTS 2.1 HINGES A. Butt hinges: five-knuckle, ball-bearing, template type. 1. Exterior doors: stainless steel, with nonremovable pins. 2. Interior doors: steel, plated finish. 3. Size: 4-1/2 by 4-1/2 inches unless otherwise indicated. B. Continuous hinges: aluminum, geared, full height of door. 2.2 LOCKS AND LATCHES A. Mortise locks: heavy duty, with lever trim. 1. Lever design: [Return lever] [Straight lever with end cap]. 2. Backset: 2-3/4 inches. B. Cylindrical locks: heavy duty, for interior doors where mortise locks are not scheduled. 2.3 EXIT DEVICES A. Rim exit devices: touch-pad type, listed for panic hardware. 2.4 CLOSERS A. Surface closers: cast iron body, full rack-and-pinion, adjustable spring power. 1. Provide parallel arm mounting on the push side of out-swinging doors. 2. Provide back-check and delayed action on accessible doors. 2.5 HARDWARE FINISHES A. Provide the following finishes unless otherwise indicated: Item Finish BHMA code Hinges, exterior Satin stainless steel 630 Hinges, interior Satin chrome, plated 652 Locks and levers Satin chrome 626 Closers Powder coat, aluminum colour 689 B. Match finishes of items on the same door. PART 3 - EXECUTION 3.1 EXAMINATION A. Examine doors and frames for correct preparation before installing hardware. 3.2 INSTALLATION A. Mount hardware at the heights recommended by the door and hardware manufacturers. B. Install each item in accordance with its manufacturer's instructions. 1. Drill and tap for machine screws; do not use self-tapping screws on metal doors. 2. Set thresholds in a full bed of sealant. 3.3 ADJUSTING A. Adjust closers so that doors close fully from 70 degrees in at least three seconds. B. Return to the site six months after Substantial Completion and readjust each closer. END OF SECTION 08 7100
